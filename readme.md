# FNB App of the Year - Practical Tasks Repository

## Overview

This repository contains all practical tasks and challenges developed as part of the **FNB App of the Year** initiative - South Africa's premier technology competition and digital ecosystem designed to celebrate, mentor, and showcase locally developed mobile applications.

Originally known as the MTN Business App of the Year, First National Bank (FNB) took over as the headline sponsor. The initiative has evolved from a simple awards ceremony into a massive year-round platform for tech entrepreneurship and talent incubation across the African continent.
